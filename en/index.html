Two axes, not one star

You learned a lot — and did you like it?

Two questions. One tap. That's all a student does after class.

Higher means: I learned more. Further right means: I enjoyed it more. One tap never glues this into a single star — because a lesson can be hard and good at once.

How to use the square

What the school sees next

Every tap is one small colored square. Collect the whole class and you get a picture that reads in seconds. Below is a demo class — invented names, not real ratings.

What this is not

This is not a tool for evaluating or ranking teachers. A single bad vote means nothing. Votes are anonymous by design. The school sets the frequency. Demonstration data only — nothing here is deployed.
